## PHASE 1 — CLOSED ECONOMY (Current)

**Status: ACTIVE ✅**

- EBCs earned through games, videos, and daily challenges
- EBCs invested in the Beat Shop (stickers, posters, VW Beetles, badges)
- Economy is fully internal — no real monetary value
- Starting balance: 25,000 EBCs
- Legal risk: ZERO — purely gamified rewards

**Key principle:** EBCs create engagement and a sense of real value
without any financial or regulatory complexity.

**Status: PLANNED 🔜**

## PHASE 3 — TOKEN / TRADEABLE ECONOMY (Future)

**Status: EXPLORATORY 💡 — DO NOT IMPLEMENT WITHOUT LEGAL CLEARANCE**

- EBCs as a tradeable digital token (e.g. on Solana, Base, or Polygon)
- Players can trade EBCs with each other
- Possible meme coin or fan token launch
- Brand: "EBC Token" — backed by the Lovely Rita Adventures ecosystem

**⚠️ Legal requirements before Phase 3:**
1. Entertainment/IP lawyer already engaged (Apple Corps licensing)
2. Additional: crypto/fintech lawyer for token classification
3. Regulatory review: SEC (US), FCA (UK), CVM (Brazil)
4. Special caution: platform has minor users — child financial protection laws apply
5. Copyright protection of EBC concept and economy BEFORE any public token launch
6. Consider: token only available to adult accounts, minors earn but cannot trade

**Strategic positioning:**
> "The world's first music-powered learning platform with a real economy"
> — this claim becomes literally true with Phase 3.
> No other educational platform has done this. First-mover advantage is significant.
