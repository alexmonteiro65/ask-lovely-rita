# Content calendar — @ai.tech.school, first 30 days

**This is a manual posting plan, not automation.** No Buffer, Later, or any
other scheduler is connected — that would need a paid account, API keys,
and a live Instagram Business connection, none of which this project
handles (see CLAUDE.md). Post these yourself, by hand, on the dates below
or whenever suits you.

## Suggested cadence

Research on 2026 posting-time data consistently points to **Tuesday–Thursday**
as the strongest days, with **~9am** and **~7–9pm** (your local audience
time) as the strongest windows. Twice a week is a sustainable pace for a
brand-new account with 5 ready assets — don't burn through them by posting
daily. Once the account has a few weeks of real data, check Instagram
Insights and shift these times to match your actual audience, not this
generic starting point.
