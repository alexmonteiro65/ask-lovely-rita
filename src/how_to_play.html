HOW TO PLAY
LOVELY RITA ADVENTURES
Welcome love! Let me show you how to play. It is easy and lots of fun — I promise!
1
🎲 Choose Your Lad
▼
On Page 1 — press the PLAY button and watch the neon spotlight spin across the four Lads. It stops on one randomly. That is your companion for this adventure!
The four Lads are: The Smart One · The Cute One · The Quiet One · The Funny One. Each has their own score which builds up every time you play.
💡 Not happy with who you got? Press PLAY again and spin as many times as you like!
2
🌍 Spin to Choose Your World
▼
On the Choose Your World page, press the spin button to land on a world. Every visit you must spin — you cannot tap a world directly without spinning first.
🐙 Octopus's Garden — Ocean life and nature
🚀 Across the Universe — Space and science
✨ The Inner Light — World music and culture
🎠 Penny Lane — Cities and geography
🎤 Rita's World — Music and the Lads
💡 Spin as many times as you want until you land on the world you love!
3
⭐ Pick Your Level
▼
Inside each world, choose your level from the three tabs at the top of the page:
🟣 Youngsters — Ages 6–12
Images shown to help you guess. Simpler questions. Go at your own pace!
🟡 Grown Ups — Ages 13–17
Answer first, then the image is revealed. More detailed questions.
⭐ Experts — Ages 18+
No hints. No images. True knowledge required. The ultimate challenge!
💡 No penalties ever! A wrong answer earns 0 points — you never lose BeatCoins.
4
🎓 Play and Earn BeatCoins
▼
Each world has challenges to complete — answer questions, match cards, or solve puzzles. Every correct answer earns you Educational BeatCoins!
Your BeatCoins total is always visible on screen and builds up across all your sessions — day after day, month after month. They never expire!
When Rita speaks, listen carefully love — she gives you hints and tells you the answer if you get it wrong, always with an educational explanation!
💡 Inside each world, use the Choose World button to go back and spin again. The Next Page button takes you to the bonus game!
5
🎮 Bonus Games
▼
Finish the main challenge in any world and Rita unlocks a special bonus game — just for fun and extra BeatCoins!
🐙 Octopus's Garden → 🎵 The Turntable
🚀 Across the Universe → ⚡ Cosmic Flash
✨ The Inner Light → 🪷 World Beats
🎠 Penny Lane → 🚗 Park in London
🎤 Rita's World → 🎵 Tap the Beat
💡 Press Next Page inside any world to go directly to the bonus game!
6
🛍️ The Beat Shop
▼
Use your BeatCoins to invest in exclusive collectibles from The Beat Shop — tap the 💎 Collectibles button in the navigation bar.
You will find Rita posters, VW Beetles, world badges, Lad posters and more — each item is limited edition. Once they are sold out, they can only be traded between players!
💡 We say invest not spend — because collecting is always a good investment love!
7
🎬 Videos and 🎵 Music
▼
Tap the 🎬 Videos button to visit Lovely Rita Videos — educational videos linked to each world. Watch a full video and earn 50 BeatCoins! Maximum 200 BeatCoins per day from videos.
Tap the 🎵 Music button to open The Beat Jukebox — tap Choose Your Song and enjoy the Lads Rap collection while you explore!
💡 Videos unlock progressively — watch the first one to unlock the next love!
8
👤 My Adventures
▼
Tap the 👤 Profile button on Page 1 to visit My Adventures — your personal space in the app.
On the My Journey tab: see your nickname, total BeatCoins, each Lad's score, the worlds you have explored, and your collectibles collection.
On the My Records tab: see your personal leaderboard — which Lad earned the most BeatCoins and how far you have explored each world.
💡 Tap the ✏️ Change Name button to set your nickname. It is the only thing you need to type!
