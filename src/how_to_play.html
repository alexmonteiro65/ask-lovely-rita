How to Play
Lovely Rita Adventures · 5 Easy Steps
Hello love! Let me walk you through the adventure. Tap each step to learn more. And remember — you can ask me anything on the first page!
1
🎲 Pick Your Lad Companion
▼
On the first page, press the big button and watch the neon highlight spin across the four Lads. It lands on one randomly — that is your companion for the adventure! If you are not happy with who you got, just spin again as many times as you like.
💡 Tip: You can also tap any Lad directly to choose them without spinning. Each Lad has their own personality and quote!
And remember — on this same page you can Ask Rita anything! Type or speak a question about music, history, the Lads, or the app itself. Rita knows everything love!
2
🌍 Choose Your World
▼
After picking your Lad, you arrive at the Choose Your World page. Press the spin button and the worlds light up one by one until one is chosen. There are 5 magical worlds — each inspired by a famous song:
🐙 Octopus's Garden — Ocean life & nature
🚀 Across the Universe — Space & science
🪷 The Inner Light — World cultures
🌆 Penny Lane — Cities & geography
🎤 Rita's World — Music & the Lads
💡 Tip: Not happy with the world you got? Spin again — no limits!
3
⭐ Choose Your Level
▼
Inside each world, pick your level from the three tabs at the top:
🟣 Youngsters
Ages 6–12. Images shown before questions. Scrambled letters to help you spell.
🟡 Grown Ups
Ages 13–17. Images shown after you answer. Type or speak your answers.
⭐ Experts
Ages 18+. The real Fab Four fans. No hints, harder questions, bigger BeatCoins!
💡 No penalties ever! Wrong answers = 0 points. You never lose BeatCoins.
4
🎓 Play & Learn
▼
Each world has 8 questions or challenges. Answer correctly to earn Educational BeatCoins. Every question teaches you something real — about science, music, geography, or world culture.
💡 When you get something wrong, Rita always explains the correct answer with an educational fact. Learning is the real prize love!
Your BeatCoins total is always visible. They accumulate across all your games and all four Lads.
5
🎮 Unlock Your Bonus Game!
▼
Finish all 8 questions in any world and Rita unlocks a special bonus game just for you! Each world has its own bonus:
🐙 Octopus's Garden → ❌⭕ Tic-Tac-Toe Underwater
🚀 Across the Universe → 🎵 The Turntable
🪷 The Inner Light → 🎵 The Turntable
🌆 Penny Lane → 🚗 Park in London
🎤 Rita's World → 🎵 The Turntable
💡 Rita counts down 5 seconds and takes you there automatically. Or press the button to go straight away!
